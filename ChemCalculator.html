Caesar's Chemistry Calculator for SS14 Reagents & Recipes

I made this project, which has nothing to do with Tom's original work, mainly to automate my process and keep all recipes relevant, dynamic, and up to date. It's not fully finished, but I've tested it extensively and it seems to be working well! This fetches raw data from the SS14 GitHub repository. Most reagents and chemicals should be present with their corresponding recipes and steps.

Due to the automated parsing process, not everything might be perfectly accurate or 100% human-readable (like status effects), and depending on your server/branch, it might not fully apply either (though it should work for 99% of cases). This is because the raw data consists of incorrectly formatted YAML files in the SS14 source code... This formatting is not only incorrect but varies across different servers/branches (incorrect indentation, custom non-standard YAML tags, empty brackets without data, etc.), so I'm opting to only implement the "main" Wizden version here for now (subject to change).

Hope you enjoy it anyway! :p
